Please remember

The short version displayed at the gate. Both full documents follow below.

Admission to Pradera Islands Waterpark is subject to the park's General Terms and Conditions of Admission, Park Rules, Safety Requirements and Assumption of Risk.

By purchasing or accepting admission, using your RFID or wristband, and entering the waterpark, you acknowledge that you have been given the opportunity to review and agree to comply with these terms.

At a glance

  • Waterpark activities involve inherent risks, including wet and slippery surfaces, moving water, changes in speed and direction, loss of balance, and contact with other guests or surfaces.
  • Follow all posted attraction rules, health warnings, weight and age requirements, and the instructions of lifeguards and park personnel.
  • Do not use an attraction if you have a medical or physical condition for which participation is restricted or may be unsafe.
  • Parents and responsible adults must actively supervise children and minors under their care. Lifeguards do not replace parental supervision.
  • Use only attractions appropriate to your swimming ability, and comply with life-jacket requirements.
  • Dangerous behavior, horseplay, intoxication, entry into restricted areas, and disregard of safety instructions are prohibited.
  • Attractions may be temporarily suspended or closed because of weather, safety inspections, maintenance, emergencies or operational requirements.
  • Guests are responsible for safeguarding their personal belongings.
  • Serious violations of park rules may result in removal from the park.

General Terms and Conditions of Admission, Park Rules, Safety Requirements and Assumption of Risk

Ticketing, admission, safety, refunds, conduct and photography · Version 1.0 · Effective 10 November 2026

PLEASE READ BEFORE BUYING A TICKET OR ENTERING. These Terms apply to Pradera Islands Water Park. The Guest Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk Agreement and all attraction-specific safety signs should also be read. Printed copies or assistance are available at Guest Services.

1. Operator, scope, and definitions

Pradera Islands Water Park (the "Park") is operated by LPM Management & Marketing Corporation ("Pradera," "we," "us," or the "Operator"). These Terms apply to all guests, ticket purchasers, companions, invitees, and persons entering the Park, whether tickets are purchased directly, through Klook or another authorized seller, or admission is complimentary. "Ticket" includes an approved electronic voucher, QR code, entry credential or other authorized booking confirmation. "RFID" refers to the wristband or credential used for Park admission or other enabled services.

2. Access to these Terms and acknowledgment at entry

These Terms and the separate Guest Liability Waiver are made available before admission through the Park's posted QR code and by request at Guest Services. They should also be accessible through our direct booking flow and, as applicable, authorized third-party sales listings before purchase. Guests should review them before buying tickets and before entering.

Subject to applicable law and adequate notice, a guest who knowingly taps an RFID wristband and enters the Park acknowledges these Terms and the Guest Liability Waiver by conduct. Pradera may keep relevant admission logs and version records as evidence of the applicable terms, subject to data-protection requirements.

Purchasers arranging admission for others must bring these Terms to their attention. Parents, legal guardians, or persons legally exercising parental authority who bring minors into the Park and knowingly tap their own RFID card or wristband and enter, after a reasonable opportunity to review these Terms and the Guest Liability Waiver, are deemed to accept both documents for themselves and, to the extent permitted by law, on behalf of the minors accompanying them. By entering, they also consent to the minors' admission and participation in permitted Park activities and agree to comply with all applicable supervision and safety requirements. Nothing in these Terms waives rights that cannot legally be waived.

3. Tickets, prices, inclusions, and authorized sellers

Ticket prices, visit dates, age bands, eligibility, operating hours and included services are those clearly stated in the applicable offer or booking confirmation at the time of purchase, subject to mandatory law. Special events, meals, lockers, rentals, premium experiences and other goods and services are separately charged.

Tickets should be purchased only from Pradera or authorized distribution partners. Tickets obtained through unauthorized resale, fraudulent means or misuse may be declined after reasonable verification. Pradera may reasonably require proof of purchase, identity, eligibility for a discounted category, or another valid admission credential.

4. Ticket validity, date changes, and transfers

A ticket is valid only for the date, category, inclusions and other conditions identified in the relevant purchase terms. Rebooking or changes requested for personal convenience are subject to the disclosed conditions for that ticket type and available capacity. Tickets and RFID wristbands must not be copied, altered, resold or transferred in violation of disclosed ticket rules. Where a permitted transfer or correction is needed, the guest should contact the original seller or Guest Services before use.

5. Admission, RFID wristbands, and re-entry

Guests must complete applicable registration, ticket validation and RFID issuance or activation before entering. Each entry credential is for the authorized holder and should not be shared. Lost, damaged or malfunctioning wristbands must be promptly reported so the credential can be checked or deactivated.

Admission is subject to capacity limits, operating conditions and lawful safety screening. Re-entry is not allowed.

6. Operating hours, attraction availability, and closures

The Park aims to provide the attractions and facilities described in the guest's purchased offer. Operating hours and access to specific attractions may nevertheless be affected by weather, lightning, water-quality concerns, maintenance, safety conditions, emergency response, capacity restrictions, regulatory requirements or events beyond reasonable control. An attraction will not operate when its use is considered unsafe.

Temporary closure of an individual attraction does not automatically entitle a guest to a full ticket refund where the Park substantially delivers the advertised experience, subject to applicable law and what was promised at sale. Where the Park cannot open, closes materially early, or fails to deliver a substantial part of the paid-for service, Pradera will assess appropriate remedies.

7. Cancellations, refunds, and booking channels

For a change of mind, late arrival, no-show or other guest-initiated cancellation not caused by Pradera, the cancellation and rebooking conditions shown before purchase will ordinarily apply, to the extent lawful.

For tickets bought through Klook or another authorized seller, guests should normally submit changes and refund requests through the issuing platform using the process disclosed in their booking. Pradera will coordinate with that partner when the reason concerns Park operations, service non-delivery or guest safety, and this process will not remove statutory rights or remedies. For tickets purchased directly from Pradera, contact Guest Services at sales@praderaislands.com.

Guests should retain their booking confirmation and relevant transaction details.

8. Compliance with attraction-specific safety guidelines

All guests must read and follow the rules and warnings displayed at each slide, pool and attraction, as well as reasonable instructions of lifeguards, ride operators, security personnel and authorized staff. Posted requirements may include height, weight, age, health, swimming ability, rider position, number of riders, prohibited items, use of mats or tubes, and other attraction-specific restrictions. The applicable posted sign and the operator's safety instructions govern participation in that attraction.

For safety reasons, Pradera may temporarily stop an attraction or refuse participation where a guest fails to meet the applicable restrictions, cannot safely follow instructions, is impaired, or creates a risk to others. Guests should not enter restricted or staff-only areas.

9. Children, guardians, and supervision

Parents, legal guardians and accompanying responsible adults must ensure children in their care comply with the rules applicable to each facility and attraction. A responsible adult must provide supervision appropriate to the child's age, swimming ability and the Park's requirements, even when lifeguards are on duty. Where specific rules require an accompanying adult, the child may not participate without one. Acceptance by a parent or guardian on behalf of accompanying minors is addressed in Section 2 above.

If a child becomes separated from an accompanying adult, Park staff may apply the missing-child protocol and take reasonable measures to reunite them. Assistance can be requested immediately from any authorized staff member.

10. Health, hygiene, and swimming safety

Guests must judge their own fitness to participate and observe medical or physical restrictions posted at attractions. Do not use an attraction when affected by a condition, injury, illness or impairment that makes use unsafe. Guests should maintain appropriate personal hygiene and follow showering, pool-use and sanitation instructions. Persons with conditions that may pose an immediate health or safety risk to themselves or others may be restricted from the affected area where lawful and reasonably necessary.

Guests must not run on wet walkways, push others, engage in horseplay, misuse rescue equipment, enter pools outside designated areas or ignore directions to exit the water. Any injury, near miss, unsafe condition or suspected contamination should be reported promptly to staff.

11. Food, restaurants, and allergens

Food and beverage outlets operate subject to posted menus, prices, availability and applicable food-safety requirements. Guests should notify restaurant staff of known allergies and ask about ingredients before ordering. While staff may provide available information, the Park cannot guarantee a particular food is free from all allergens unless expressly confirmed by the relevant operator. Bringing outside food, drinks, glass containers or other items is not allowed.

12. Guest conduct and prohibited activities

  • Be respectful to staff and other guests. No violence, harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, intimidation or deliberate damage to property.
  • No dangerous conduct, queue jumping that creates safety risks, tampering with equipment, climbing barriers, misuse of attractions or entering restricted spaces.
  • No illegal drugs, weapons, hazardous items or glass in areas where prohibited for safety. Alcohol consumption and smoking/vaping are prohibited within the Park.
  • Wear appropriate attire and footwear where required and comply with instructions concerning loose articles, jewelry, phones and unsecured equipment.
  • Pets are not allowed in the Park.
  • All entering guests shall abide by the rules on restricted and prohibited items.
  • Refrain from conduct that unreasonably intrudes on others' privacy or prevents their safe and comfortable use of the Park.

Where reasonably necessary and consistent with law, Pradera may warn a guest, stop an unsafe activity, refuse access to an attraction, or require a guest to leave.

13. Security, safety screening, and emergency instructions

Reasonable security and bag-screening procedures may be applied for safety, in a lawful, proportionate and non-discriminatory manner. Guests must comply with emergency announcements, evacuation directions and instructions from authorized personnel. Pradera may provide or summon available first aid and emergency assistance.

14. Photography, filming, and guest-generated content

Personal photos and videos may be taken in areas where permitted, provided filming does not interfere with safety, staff duties or others' privacy. Photography or recording is prohibited in toilets, changing rooms and other clearly designated private or restricted areas. Cameras, mounts, drones and other equipment may not be used on attractions or in other areas where prohibited by posted signs or staff directions. Commercial shoots, organized influencer activities and advertising productions require prior written permission from Pradera and any other necessary permissions.

Photography and filming may take place in designated areas of Pradera Islands Waterpark for promotional use on its official channels, advertisements, and partner publications, subject to applicable privacy laws.

Guests must respect the privacy and intellectual-property rights of others.

15. CCTV, personal information, and third-party platforms

Pradera may process limited personal information for ticketing, admission, safety, security, customer support, incident response, accounting and other lawful purposes. CCTV may operate in appropriately designated public or semi-public areas for legitimate security and safety purposes. Where applicable, booking and payment partners process information under their own notices and contractual roles.

16. Personal property, lockers, and lost items

Guests should keep valuables secured and use any available storage facilities according to posted instructions. Guests should report missing property to Guest Services. To the extent permitted by law, Pradera is not responsible for losses arising solely from guests' own failure to safeguard belongings, but nothing here excludes responsibility established under applicable law. Found items are handled under the Park's applicable retention and return procedures.

17. Damage to property and guest responsibility

Guests may be held responsible, in accordance with law, for damage or loss directly caused by their intentional misconduct or negligent misuse of Park property. Pradera may seek lawful remedies for fraud, theft, vandalism or other prohibited acts.

18. Complaints, incident reports, and requests for assistance

Please report injuries, unsafe conditions, failed ticket scans, billing concerns, staff misconduct or other incidents promptly to Guest Services or an authorized manager. Where possible, provide the date and time, relevant ticket or RFID reference, location and a short description so the matter can be investigated. Pradera may preserve relevant records for safety, legal compliance, insurance or legitimate claims, subject to applicable law.

19. Liability and the separate Assumption of Risk Agreement

Water attractions involve ordinary inherent risks. Guests must comply with the separate Guest Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk Agreement, which explains those risks and the guest's voluntary participation. Nothing in these Terms waives rights or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be waived, including liability arising from negligence, gross negligence, willful misconduct, statutory violations or failure to comply with applicable safety duties.

20. Promotions, special events, and third-party services

Special offers, vouchers, group arrangements, hosted events and third-party services may have additional written conditions, provided these are made accessible before the relevant transaction and do not override mandatory rights. Any authorized third-party seller's booking mechanics should be read together with these Terms. Where terms conflict, Pradera commercial terms apply to the extent lawful and consistent with what was actually sold.

21. Changes to these Terms, severability, and governing law

Pradera may update these Terms prospectively for operational, safety, legal or commercial reasons. We will identify the applicable version and effective date and provide reasonable notice of material changes. Changes will not retroactively remove accrued customer rights or materially reduce promised benefits for tickets already purchased, except where required by law or reasonably necessary for safety, with appropriate remedies where applicable. Immediate safety rules and staff instructions remain enforceable for current conditions.

If any provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining lawful provisions continue to apply. Philippine law governs these Terms, without prejudice to the rights of consumers to avail themselves of legally available complaint and dispute-resolution processes.

Guest Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk Agreement

Applies to paid and complimentary guests · Version 1.0 · Effective 10 November 2026

IMPORTANT: Please read this document and the Park General Terms & Conditions of Admission, Park Rules, Safety Requirements and Assumption of Risk before entering. If you do not understand a provision, ask Guest Services for an explanation or a printed copy before tapping your RFID wristband.

1. Who this agreement covers

This document applies to each person entering or using Pradera Islands Water Park (the "Park"), operated by LPM Management & Marketing Corporation ("Pradera" or the "Operator"), whether admission is paid, complimentary, or obtained through an authorized ticketing partner. "Attractions" includes pools, water slides, play areas, and other water-based activities offered at the Park.

2. Nature of the activities and inherent risks

I understand that visiting a water park and using its attractions involve physical activity and inherent risks. These can include slipping or falling on wet surfaces; sudden movement, acceleration or impact; collision with a slide, equipment or another person; loss of balance; accidental injury; immersion in water, difficulty swimming, and drowning. Weather, crowd movement and other guests' actions may also affect safety. Not every risk can be eliminated even where reasonable precautions are taken.

Naiintindihan ko na may kaakibat na panganib ang paglangoy at paggamit ng water slides at iba pang attractions. Kusang-loob akong sasali at susunod sa mga safety rules.

3. Health, suitability, and the right not to participate

I am responsible for deciding whether I can safely participate and for checking the restrictions applicable to each attraction. I will not use an attraction if I have a medical, physical or other condition that makes it unsafe for me, or if I do not meet its posted requirements. Certain attractions may not be appropriate for persons who are pregnant; have heart, blood-pressure, neck, back or other conditions; have undergone recent surgery; or otherwise require medical clearance. I will ask Park staff about safety requirements where uncertain. I understand that I may choose not to participate in any attraction.

4. Compliance with Park and attraction-specific safety rules

I will comply with the Park Terms & Conditions, all posted signs, and instructions of lifeguards, slide operators, security personnel and other authorized staff. These include applicable height, weight, age, rider-position, swimwear and health restrictions; instructions on flotation devices; and rules governing queues, entry and exit from each attraction. I will not run, push, dive into prohibited areas, ride in a prohibited manner, bypass a safety device, or engage in unsafe behavior. I will not use an attraction while impaired by alcohol, medication, or drugs.

Specific rules displayed at individual attractions form part of the applicable safety requirements. If a sign or instruction is unclear, I will ask an authorized staff member before riding.

5. Children and other persons requiring assistance

A parent or lawful guardian must ensure that a minor under their supervision meets each attraction's requirements and follows all safety instructions. Minors and persons who require assistance must be accompanied or supervised as required by the Park's posted rules and staff directions. Lifeguards and Park personnel do not replace the responsibility of an accompanying adult to supervise children in their care.

The parent or lawful guardian should complete the Park's designated guardian acknowledgment at registration or through the approved ticketing process. A minor's RFID tap alone is not treated as a parent's signature or as a waiver of the minor's legal rights.

6. Safety decisions and emergency assistance

Pradera may delay, suspend, restrict or stop the use of an attraction, or refuse entry or continued participation, when reasonably necessary for safety, operational or lawful reasons. Staff may request that a guest leave an attraction or an unsafe area. In an emergency, the Park may call first responders or arrange available first aid, and may share necessary information with responders in accordance with applicable law. This does not replace professional medical assessment or treatment.

7. Voluntary participation and limited assumption of risk

By choosing to enter and participate, I voluntarily accept the ordinary and inherent risks described above insofar as they arise without fault or breach of legal duty by Pradera. To the extent permitted by Philippine law, I agree not to make claims against Pradera, its officers, employees and authorized representatives solely for injuries or losses resulting from such inherent risks, where Pradera has acted lawfully and with due care.

Nothing in this Agreement waives or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be waived, including liability arising from negligence, gross negligence, willful misconduct, violation of law or failure to comply with applicable safety duties.

8. Personal belongings and responsible conduct

I will take reasonable care of my belongings, secure loose items before using attractions, and observe any restrictions on jewelry, phones, cameras and other objects. Pradera's responsibility for loss or damage will be determined under applicable law and the circumstances of the incident; no absolute exclusion of legal responsibility is intended.

9. Privacy, photography, and separate permissions

I understand that guest registration, admission records and CCTV may be processed for admission, safety, security, incident response and other lawful purposes under Pradera's Privacy Notice. Any separate promotional or commercial use requiring consent will be addressed under a distinct authorization or another applicable lawful basis.

10. How this agreement is acknowledged

This Agreement and the Park Terms & Conditions are available through the QR code displayed before entry and through Guest Services. A guest who has been given a fair opportunity to read them and then knowingly taps an RFID wristband and enters the Park acknowledges these posted terms through that conduct, to the extent permitted by law.

Kung may hindi malinaw, magtanong muna sa Guest Services bago pumasok. Huwag i-tap ang RFID at pumasok kung hindi ka sang-ayon sa mga tuntunin; assistance regarding ticket remedies is available under the Park Terms & Conditions and applicable law.

11. Relationship to other rules; governing law

This Agreement should be read with the Park General Terms & Conditions of Admission, Park Rules, Safety Requirements and Assumption of Risk and current attraction signs. If a mandatory law gives a guest greater protection, that law prevails. If any part is held invalid, the remainder continues to apply to the extent lawful. Philippine law governs this Agreement.

12. Contact and availability of a copy

Questions or requests for a copy may be directed to Guest Services at the Park, or by email to sales@praderaislands.com. Document version: 1.0. Effective date: 10 November 2026.
